Hello, and why I'm starting this blog

Anjali Parihar, 5 October 2026

I learn best by building things, and I remember them best when I explain them. So this blog is where I'll do both.

What I'll write about

Mostly three things:

  • Industrial data: what real factory and sensor data looks like, and how to make it useful.
  • Practical AI: anomaly detection, RAG, AI agents and the trade-offs that only show up once you build them.
  • Learning in public: new tools and ideas I'm exploring, including Physical AI.

Why write at all

Writing forces me to check whether I really understand something. If I can't explain it simply, I don't understand it yet.

If any of these topics interest you, feel free to reach out. I'd love to hear what you're working on.
